About GIF Forge

Everything runs on your machine. Video frames are decoded with the HTML5 <video> and Canvas APIs, GIFs are decoded with omggif, encoded with gif.js in Web Workers, and zipped with JSZip.

Tips

  • Shorter clips at 480p keep GIF files small — 720p+ GIFs get huge fast.
  • Dithering helps gradients but increases file size by roughly a third.
  • Use the timeline handles to trim, then press Space to preview the loop.
  • Fit to a size budget walks frame rate, width and quality down until the estimate fits your platform's limit.
  • Frame extraction converts each frame to a Blob, so hundreds of frames stay light on memory. Shift+click selects a whole range of frames.
